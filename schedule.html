    PS Sides: Zoe, Hugo, Binal & Orin

    Social engineering and AI

    They’re four friends, aged 11 and 12, from a primary school in Perth. They’re curious, creative, and always up for trying something new. Like most kids their age, their interests stretch in all different directions – they’re even busy working together on their first movie. For the first time, they’re stepping onto a conference stage together, having chosen to challenge themselves by learning about cyber security. They might see things a little differently from the usual BSides Perth speaker, and that’s exactly the point: the digital world that you are designing, securing and building today is the one they’re growing up in, and so it’s a world where their views matter.
